# 00｜全話共通設定

全10話のすべてのショットに適用する。各話の制作台本より上位。

---

## SERIES STYLE — 全ショットの冒頭に貼る

```
SERIES STYLE (apply to every shot):
Premium Japanese cinematic biography, photorealistic, vertical 9:16.
Shot on a full-frame cinema camera with prime lenses, shallow depth of field, subtle natural film grain, muted desaturated color grade with warm practical light sources against cool ambient shadow.
Performances are emotionally restrained at all times. Nobody in this series cries loudly, gestures broadly, or looks at the camera unless explicitly instructed.
Camera movement is minimal and motivated: a slow push-in, a slow lateral dolly, or a locked-off frame. Never use whip pans, orbits, drone moves, speed ramps or slow motion.
Every environment must read as authentically Japanese for its stated decade. No Western interiors, no generic international offices, no stock-footage business imagery.
This is one person's life, not an advertisement.
```

## LOCATION LOCK — 同じ場所は同じ記述で

```
LOC-DANCHI (1980s public housing apartment — episodes 1 and 2 only):
a six-tatami room with a wooden study desk, an old bookshelf and a sliding paper screen door; a narrow hallway; a small kitchen with a gas range. Only these three angles. Late-afternoon slanted sunlight by day, a single dim ceiling bulb at night.

LOC-APARTMENT (1990s rented flat — episodes 3 to 6):
a cramped one-room flat with cardboard boxes, a low table, a futon and a small kitchenette. Fluorescent tube light at night, flat overcast light by day.

LOC-DRESSING (club dressing room — episodes 4 to 6):
a narrow room with a bulb-lit mirror, cluttered cosmetics, dresses on a rail.

LOC-ALLEY (the fixed Ginza back street — episodes 1, 7, 8, 10):
the same narrow street with closed shutters, wet asphalt and a slim three-storey building. Always shot from the same two angles so the viewer recognises it.

LOC-GINZAKURABU (high-end Ginza club — episodes 7 to 10):
mahogany walls, low warm practicals, crystal glassware, a small stage of bottles behind the bar.

LOC-TEMPLE (episode 10 only):
the wooden veranda of a small rural temple at dawn, shoji screens, a broom, a swept stone path.
```

## THIRD PARTY RULE — 全話で厳守

```
THIRD PARTY RULE:
The father, the mother, the husband, the boyfriends and the men who defrauded her are real living people who have not consented to appear. They must never be shown with a visible face.
Represent them only through: a back, hands, a mouth in profile behind a glass, footsteps, a voice, or an object they have just left.
Identify them by clothing colour instead of by face — the father always in a white dress shirt, the mother always in a mustard-yellow cardigan.
Never state their names, employers, districts or affiliations.
Violence is never shown. Show only the moment before and the moment after.
```

## 生成設定

- aspect_ratio: 9:16 を毎回明示する
- 年代別の基準キーフレームを静止画で確定してから、それを start_image にして image-to-video で動かす
- text-to-video で人物を直接出さない
- 1ショット＝1アクション。1本のプロンプトに複数の場面転換を詰め込まない

## ナレーション共通仕様

- 女性、落ち着いた低めのトーン、ドキュメンタリーの語り
- 感情は間・呼吸・速度で作る。声量で作らない
- 一文ごとに速度を変える。一定速度で読ませない
- 感情の頂点では速度を上げない（0.95〜1.10倍）
- 誤読リスクのある語だけひらがなに開く
- 制作順序：音声を先に生成して実測し、その秒数で映像を生成する

| 内容 | 速度 |
|---|---|
| 話の導入 | 1.10〜1.20倍 |
| 事実・数字の提示 | 1.30〜1.40倍 |
| 反転・引きの直前 | 1.10〜1.20倍 |
| 説明・経過 | 1.35〜1.45倍 |
| 感情の頂点（開示・喪失・決断） | 0.95〜1.10倍 |
| 視聴者への語りかけ・CTA | 1.05〜1.15倍 |
